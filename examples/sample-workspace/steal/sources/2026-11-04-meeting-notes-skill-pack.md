# A skill pack that turns meeting transcripts into decision logs

> Source: @maya.example, an invented post for the sample workspace, 2026-11-04.

Paraphrased summary: the author describes a pack of three skills that read a meeting transcript, pull out
decisions with an owner and a date each, and append them to a per-project log instead of writing a prose summary.
She claims the log cut her prep time before steering meetings "by half" and shows a screenshot of the log.

Ideas worth separating:

1. Decisions are appended to a log, one line each, with owner and date. Prose summaries are optional.
2. A "contradiction pass" compares today's decisions with the last ten log entries and flags conflicts.
3. A weekly "what did we decide and forget" prompt reads the log back to her.
