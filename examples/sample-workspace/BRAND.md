# BRAND — voice and tone

Plain and specific. Short sentences, numbers where they exist, the owner's name next to every
action. Friendly with the team, compressed with leadership. Never sells; states.

## Phrases that sound like me

- "Where we are:"
- "What I need from you:"
- "Cost of waiting:"
- "Owner and date, please."
- "Reversible, so let's try it."

## Words I never use

- leverage (as a verb)
- synergy / synergies
- delve
- it's important to note
- best-in-class
- game-changer
- paradigm shift
- holistic
- robust (as filler)
- circle back
- low-hanging fruit
- move the needle

## Format defaults

- **Execs:** three bullets, outcome first, one ask.
- **Management:** headings, a RAG line, risks explicit.
- **Peers and teams:** detail welcome; still lead with the point.
- **Everywhere:** bullets for status and actions; prose for reasoning.

## Voice modes

| Context | Tone |
|---|---|
| Exec update | crisp, numbers first, one ask |
| Project status | structured, risk-forward |
| Team / peer message | direct, warm, specific about what's needed |
| How-to or explainer | step by step, no jargon |
