# Fernhill Product Framework: P4 Principal PM

## Discovery Craft

- `p4-disc-opportunity-sizing` — Sizes and sequences opportunities across several product areas.
- `p4-disc-customer-voice` — Is the person other teams ask for the customer view on a topic.

## Delivery Rhythm

- `p4-del-programme-shape` — Shapes a programme of work spanning more than one quarter and several teams.

## Influence and Narrative

- `p4-inf-company-narrative` — Shapes a narrative that leadership adopts company-wide.
- `p4-inf-external-presence` — Represents the product externally: customers, partners, events.

## Evidence and Metrics

- `p4-evi-measurement-system` — Builds a measurement approach that other teams reuse.

## Team Uplift

- `p4-team-org-multiplier` — Changes how a wider group works, through coaching, sessions or tooling.
- `p4-team-growth-pipeline` — Grows two or more PMs who step up under their guidance.
