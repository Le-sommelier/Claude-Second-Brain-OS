## How I work

I decide early and revisit when the data moves; a reversible call today beats a perfect one next
month. I want the risk before the good news. I write things down so the team doesn't have to ask
me, and I expect owners and dates on every action. I lose patience with status updates that
describe activity instead of outcomes.
