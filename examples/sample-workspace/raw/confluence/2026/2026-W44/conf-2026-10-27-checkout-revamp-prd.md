# Checkout revamp PRD

## Summary

The checkout revamp replaces the three-step checkout with a single page that keeps the address form first and shows saved payment methods below it.

## Goals

- Reduce checkout abandonment for returning customers.
- Cut the average time from cart to confirmation.

## Scope

In scope: address form, payment step, order confirmation. Out of scope: loyalty redemption.

## Open questions

- Saved-card list position (decided for the pilot: below the address form).
