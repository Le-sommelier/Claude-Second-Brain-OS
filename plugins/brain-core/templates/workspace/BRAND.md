## Format defaults

- **Execs:** short, structured, outcome first. Three bullets per point at most.
- **Management:** moderate length, clear headings, risks explicit.
- **Peers and teams:** can go longer; detail welcome, still structured.
- **Everywhere:** bullets over paragraphs for status and actions.
