# Output style — exec update

Use for a steering group, a monthly programme update, or any note to senior leadership.

## Audience contract

- They read for **90 seconds** at most.
- They want **outcomes**, not process.
- They want **what's hard and what you're asking of them**, not what went well.
- They read the last update, so don't restate what hasn't changed.

## Rules

- **Three bullets per section at most.** If you have four, you have two.
- **Lead with the verb or the decision.** Never "We have been working on...".
- **Quantify.** "Down 12%", "by Friday", "two weeks late"; not "significantly", "soon".
- **One ask per update.** If there are two, number them and give each an owner.
- **No unrequested context.** No methodology unless asked.
- **No status colour change without evidence.** Reuse the last colours unless something concrete moved; cite it.
- **Trend beside colour**: up / flat / down is more useful than the colour alone.

## Default shapes

**Status (one section):** headline status (colour + trend, one line); the top three things that moved; the top risk and the ask.

**Ask (one section):** what changes; why now (one line); cost and benefit (one line each); decision needed by <date>, owner <name>.

**Decision needed (one section):** the decision in one sentence; options A and B (two lines each, recommendation marked); the cost of waiting (one line).

## Banned for this audience

- Acronyms not defined earlier in the update
- Methodology talk ("we ran five interviews")
- Internal team names leadership won't recognise
- Appendix material nobody will refer to
