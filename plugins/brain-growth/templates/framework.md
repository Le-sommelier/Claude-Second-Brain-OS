# Fernhill Product Framework: P3 Senior PM

> Replace this with your company's framework. Format rules: frontmatter gives the level, optional track and version; each `## Skill` heading is a skill; each bullet is "- `criterion-id` — criterion text". IDs are stable references: reword the text freely, never recycle an ID.

## Discovery Craft

- `p3-disc-problem-framing` — Frames problems in customer terms before proposing solutions.
- `p3-disc-research-habit` — Runs or commissions customer research every quarter.

## Delivery Rhythm

- `p3-del-plan-to-ship` — Takes a multi-squad initiative from plan to release without escalation.
- `p3-del-risk-flagging` — Flags risks early with options attached.

## Influence and Narrative

- `p3-inf-exec-narrative` — Writes decision papers that senior leaders act on.
- `p3-inf-cross-team` — Aligns other teams without line authority.

## Evidence and Metrics

- `p3-evi-metric-ownership` — Defines and instruments the success metrics for their area.
- `p3-evi-experiments` — Designs experiments and acts on the result.

## Team Uplift

- `p3-team-coaching` — Coaches a less experienced PM to a visible step up.
- `p3-team-shared-tooling` — Shares methods or tooling that other teams adopt.
